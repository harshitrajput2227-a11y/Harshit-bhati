# AI Career Resilience in India: A Skill-Based Analysis of Job Transformation Toward 2030

## Overview
This research examines how artificial intelligence may affect careers 
in India by 2030 — not through the lens of job loss, but through 
career resilience. An original AI Career Resilience Index was developed 
to evaluate nine occupational categories across five dimensions.

## Key Findings
- 55.6% of careers analyzed fall in the high resilience category
- Resilience correlates with human interaction and decision-making complexity
- India's near-term AI challenge is digitization, not advanced automation
